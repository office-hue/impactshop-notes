Donations on Sharity – NGO guide

Sharity generates donations in multiple ways for your organization: purchases, video views, completing tasks, and filling out surveys. It does not cost your supporters anything – revenue comes from merchants and advertisers.

This page focuses on the Impact Shop (affiliate shopping) and the Tasks channel (offerwall, surveys).

📊 Donation channels

Two main channels drive recurring donations for NGOs.

🛒

🛒 Impact Shop – donations from shopping

The Impact Shop is a simple flow: a supporter selects your NGO and shops as usual. The commission becomes donation + platform revenue.

How it works

  • Supporter selects your NGO
  • Shopping happens in partner stores
  • Commission is shared between donation + platform
  • You see the totals transparently

Why it works

  • No extra cost to the supporter
  • Recurring, predictable revenue stream
  • Easy to communicate and share
  • Works with 500+ partners

💰 Where does the money come from?

Partner merchants and advertisers pay affiliate commissions and ad fees. We split this into donation + platform operations.

Example

10,000 HUF purchase → 5% commission = 500 HUF. If your NGO is Rising (50%) and the user is Gold (1.1×): 500 × 50% × 1.1 = 275 HUF donation.

🎁 Level bonuses – more impact at higher levels

User levels increase donation multipliers. This incentivizes long-term engagement and brings more to your NGO.

Basic1.00×Base donation level
Bronze1.05×Starter bonus
Silver1.10×Steady supporter
Gold1.15×High activity
Platinum1.20×Top engagement
Legend1.25×Community leader

⏱️ Guidance: Bronze ~1–2 weeks, Silver ~3–5 weeks, Gold ~1–2 months, Platinum ~2–4 months, Legend ~4–6 months with steady activity.

What it takes for each level
  • Bronze: ~1–2 weeks with regular activity
  • Silver: ~1 month with 5+ active days/week
  • Gold: ~1–2 months for active users
  • Platinum: ~2–4 months with consistent daily use
  • Legend: ~4–6 months for the most active users
With occasional use, Platinum/Legend are typically not reachable.

📋 Tasks – Offerwall & Surveys

Offerwall activities include surveys, registrations, app trials and other CPA tasks. Each completion generates points and donations.

What counts as a task?

  • Surveys and questionnaires
  • App installs and trials
  • Newsletter signups
  • Partner engagements

Why it matters

  • New donation pool revenue
  • Fast points and votes for users
  • High-margin advertiser payouts
  • Scales with your community

🏪 Impact Shop partner webshops

We work with 500+ online and offline partners. Your NGO can promote the Impact Shop link and earn from everyday shopping.

📊 What do you see on the dashboard?

NGO dashboards show donations, activity, votes, and trends. You can export data and share live progress with supporters.

🚀 How to get more donations

Communicate regularly

Share your NGO link weekly on Facebook, email and groups. Repetition brings results.

Offer clear actions

Tell supporters what to do: shop, watch, and complete tasks. Provide direct links.

Show progress

Update your community with donation totals and milestones. Transparency motivates action.

⏱️ Payout flow

Donations are tracked continuously and paid out according to the Impact Shop payout schedule. You will always see the latest totals.
