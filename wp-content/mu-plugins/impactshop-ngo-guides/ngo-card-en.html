NGO Card embed guide

This is the canonical guide for placing the Sharity NGO Card on your nonprofit's website. The component loads live NGO data from Sharity's API and renders a public-facing card with your name, current amounts, status and supporter actions.

When to use it

Choose the NGO Card if you want a lightweight Sharity presence on your own website without sending visitors through a longer product explainer first.

How it fits into the guide system

The NGO Card is the easiest website integration. If you also want donation growth guides, continue with Impact Shop for shopping-based donations or Impact Challenge for video, offerwall and voting-based support.

Embed in 2 steps

1) Load the script

<script async src="https://app.sharity.hu/wp-content/mu-plugins/impactshop-ngo-card.js"></script>

2) Add the card container (NGO slug)

<div data-ngo-card="YOUR-SLUG" variant="full"></div>

The slug is your NGO's identifier (as used in Sharity links). If you are unsure, email office@sharity.hu.

Variants

Use the variant attribute to switch layouts:

  • full – detailed (default)
  • compact – compact
  • widget – small widget layout

Technical note: the card calls /wp-json/impact/v1/ngo-card/<slug>.

Common issues

  • 404 / empty card: check the slug and whether the script loads.
  • CSP blocked: allow scripts and API calls to https://app.sharity.hu on your site.
  • Multiple cards: you can place multiple data-ngo-card blocks on one page.
