📊 Direct Partner settlement - example

You decide how much of the commission you allocate to Sharity. The amount is split automatically:

Commission split:
  • 40-60% - Donation to NGO (by NGO level: Base 40% · Rising 50% · Legend 60%)
  • Remaining share - Sharity platform fee

User level bonus (Basic ×1.00 → Legend ×1.25) multiplies the NGO donation share. Example: Legend NGO 60% × Legend user ×1.25 = 75% donation share. Sharity covers the difference.

💡 Customer discounts are set separately, not deducted from commission.

Example (2% commission on 10,000 HUF purchase):
  • 10,000 HUF × 2% = 200 HUF commission
  • Base NGO: 80 HUF donation · 120 HUF Sharity
  • Rising NGO: 100 HUF donation · 100 HUF Sharity
  • Legend NGO: 120 HUF donation · 80 HUF Sharity
  • + Legend user bonus (×1.25): donation can reach 75% of commission (Sharity covers)

⭐ Higher NGO level = higher donation share and user level bonus (up to ×1.25) further increases it - at Sharity's expense, not yours.

❓ FAQ

Which integration should I choose?

Already on an affiliate network → Affiliate Partner (fastest)
Brand awareness or campaigns → Sponsor
Own system + discounts → Direct Partner
Need consumer insights → Offerwall Partner

How much does it cost?

Affiliate and direct integration are free. No fixed fee, you pay only after transactions (rate set by you). Sponsor and offerwall placements have custom pricing.

What does "free ad inventory" mean?

Direct partners get free banner/video placement in Impact Challenge. For affiliate partners, visibility is funded from commission. Sponsor-only placements are priced separately.

How much donation is generated?

Affiliate: part of commission becomes donation automatically.
Direct: you decide the percentage. Donation share is 40-60% based on NGO level, and user multiplier (×1.00 → ×1.25) scales it further - e.g. Legend NGO 60% × Legend user ×1.25 = 75% donation.

How do I see results?

Track transactions, conversions, and impact in the partner dashboard. Direct partners get full analytics, affiliate partners get network dashboards plus Sharity reports.

What companies can join?

Any Hungarian webshop, offline store, service provider, or brand - ecommerce, hospitality, accommodation, beauty, fitness, retail, and more. Webshop is not required: restaurants, stores, salons, hotels can also join. Requirement: legal and ethical operation.
