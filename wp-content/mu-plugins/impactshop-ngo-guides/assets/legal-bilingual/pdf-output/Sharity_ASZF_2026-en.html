4. Description of Services

4.1 Impact Shop

4.1.1. The Impact Shop offers affiliate links to 500+ partner stores. The User shops at the regular price; the store pays an affiliate commission, a portion of which is directly converted into a donation for the NGO chosen by the User.

4.1.2. The size of the donation depends on the following factors: - (a) The affiliate commission rate (varies by store, typically 2–15%); - (b) The NGO's tier: Base (40%), Rising (50%), Legend (60%); - (c) The donation multiplier corresponding to the User's Level/Tier (Basic ×1.00 – Legend ×1.25).

4.1.3. The donation multiplier differential is borne by the Service Provider from its own share — it entails no additional cost to the User and no additional fee to the Partner.

4.1.4. In addition to the purchase, the User also receives Points and Votes.

4.2 Impact Challenge

4.2.1. In the Impact Challenge, the User may: - (a) watch short (max. 30-second) sponsor videos; - (b) complete Offerwall tasks (filling out surveys, trying out applications, sign-ups); - (c) earn Points for other activities (daily login, completing profile, sharing the page, etc.).

4.2.2. Video watching and task completion are free of charge for the User. Revenue is derived from advertisers and Sponsors.

4.2.3. Streak bonus: activity on consecutive days provides a multiplier on Points and Votes (7 days: ×1.10; 14 days: ×1.20; 30 days: ×1.30). The Streak does not apply to Sponsor videos.

4.3 Impact Amplifier (Donations)

4.3.1. Through the Impact Amplifier feature, the User may make a donation to the Donation Organizer and receive Votes immediately in return.

4.3.2. Vote packages:

Package Donation Votes Bonus

4.3.3. Distribution of the donation: - 50% → Common Donation Pool (distributed quarterly among the NGOs in proportion to Votes); - 50% → Platform maintenance cost (the Service Provider's system-usage fee, invoiced to the Donation Organizer).

In the event of a refund, the credited Votes shall be revoked.

4.5.6. Point decay: Points may decrease on a daily basis after 5 days of inactivity, but may not fall below 100 Points. Active use halts the decay.

4.6 Votes and Quarterly Distribution

4.6.1. The User may cast Votes for one or more NGOs.

4.6.2. The Common Donation Pool is distributed quarterly among the NGOs, in proportion to the Votes cast:

ngo_share = (ngo_votes / total_valid_votes) × total_pool_amount

4.6.3. Payouts are made quarterly (end of March, June, September, December). The minimum payout amount is HUF 5,000 per channel (Impact Shop direct donation, and Impact Challenge/Amplifier Common Donation Pool share, respectively). The two channels are not aggregated for the purposes of the minimum threshold. In its informational materials, the Service Provider treats the quarterly schedule as canonical; any discrepancy in publicly available guides does not alter the provisions of these GTC.
