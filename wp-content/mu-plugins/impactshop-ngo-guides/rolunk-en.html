⭐ Points and 🗳️ Votes - what do they do?

Both channels give you two rewards:

🗳️

Votes → Donations

Votes decide which NGO receives the donation from the central pool.

🏦 Central donation pool: built from revenues and donations. We distribute it quarterly to NGOs based on votes. More votes = larger NGO share.

💡 Fast votes: donate via Impact Amplifier to get 500-20,000 votes instantly.

⭐

Points → Levels → Benefits

Points are for leveling up. Higher level = 3 benefits. Your points are kept, but they can slowly decrease after long inactivity.

⏳ After a 3‑day grace period, inactivity decay starts and higher levels also have weekly natural decay. It never drops below 100 points.

🎯 Daily points cap: 1,500 points. Video types have their own daily limits.

📈 Earn points in any channel - all points add up. Level-ups are automatic once you reach a threshold.

🆔 Pseudo ID — automatic start

On your first visit, an anonymous Pseudo ID is stored in your browser and your points/votes are tied to it — so you progress without registration. If you clear browser data or switch devices, this ID can be lost, so it’s worth saving your recovery ID to link your progress later. Details: Impact Shop · Impact Challenge · NGO Card.

🎯 Summary

Leveling up is better for you (discounts) and for your NGO (stronger votes + bigger donations). The more active you are, the bigger the impact.

Point thresholds adapt to community size - values shown are current thresholds.

⏱️ Guidance: Bronze ~1–2 weeks, Silver ~3–5 weeks, Gold ~1–2 months, Platinum ~2–4 months, Legend ~4–6 months with steady activity.

What it takes for each level
  • Bronze: ~1–2 weeks with regular activity
  • Silver: ~1 month with 5+ active days/week
  • Gold: ~1–2 months for active users
  • Platinum: ~2–4 months with consistent daily use
  • Legend: ~4–6 months for the most active users
With occasional use, Platinum/Legend are typically not reachable.

❓ FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. Shopping price does not change, tasks are free. Donations come from partner commissions and ad spend.

Impact Shop vs Impact Challenge - what is the difference?

Impact Shop: discounted shopping, direct donation from commission, plus points and votes. Impact Challenge: watch videos and complete tasks - earn points and votes.

Points vs votes?

Points = levels and benefits. Votes = donation distribution from the shared pool.

How does my NGO receive donations?

We distribute the shared pool quarterly by vote share. More votes = larger donation share.

What is vote weight multiplier?

Higher levels make each vote count more. Legend = 6x.

What is the donation multiplier?

Higher level means purchases generate more donation without paying extra (e.g., Gold 1.15x).

What tasks can I complete?

Surveys, app trials, registrations. All free and safe, only verified advertisers.

Can I change my NGO?

Yes, anytime. Choose from 100+ NGOs.

What is Impact Amplifier?

Impact Amplifier lets you donate directly and receive instant votes. 50% goes to the shared pool, 50% covers operations. Votes are applied to your NGO automatically.

Are purchased votes different from free votes?

No. They count the same in distribution. Donation-based votes also grow the shared pool, so overall impact is higher.

Can I get a donation certificate?

Yes. Corporate donors can request a donation certificate during payment. Issued by Sharity Foundation.
