🎬 Video views = donations

Impact Activity - NGO Guide

Activity turns into donations: supporters watch short videos, earn votes, and allocate them to their favorite NGO. Advertisers pay for the views, NGOs receive the donation.

You participate automatically - just tell your community and collect votes.

🎬 Impact Activity How it works

🎯 In short

  • Supporters watch videos and earn votes
  • Votes are allocated to your NGO
  • Monthly donation pool is split by vote share
  • Participation is automatic and free

💰 How much does it cost?

🎁

Joining: FREE

Participation in Impact Activity is free. You only benefit - donations are driven by supporter activity.

📊

Where the money comes from

Advertisers pay for video views. A portion goes into the donation pool, which is split by vote share.

📝 Example calculation

Monthly donation pool: 500,000 HUF
Total votes: 10,000
Your votes: 1,000 (10%)
Your share: 60,000 HUF

🏆 Levels and benefits

User level affects vote weight, discount, and the donation multiplier. A Legend-level supporter vote counts 6x.

Level Point threshold Vote weight Discount Donation multiplier
🌱 Basic 0 ×1 50% ×1.00
🥉 Bronze 3,000 ×2 60% ×1.05
🥈 Silver 20,000 ×3 70% ×1.10
🥇 Gold 60,000 ×4 80% ×1.15
💎 Platinum 150,000 ×5 90% ×1.20
👑 Legend 300,000+ ×6 100% ×1.25

💡 Tip: Keep loyal, active supporters - they matter more. A Legend vote counts 6x and the donation multiplier adds +25%.

Point thresholds adapt to community size - values shown are the current thresholds.

⏱️ Guidance: Bronze in a few days, Silver ~3–4 weeks, Gold ~2–3 months, Platinum ~3–4 months, Legend ~6–7 months with steady activity.

What it takes for each level
  • Bronze: reachable in a few days of active use
  • Silver: ~1 month with regular 5+ days/week activity
  • Gold: ~2–3 months for active users
  • Platinum: ~3–4 months with consistent daily use
  • Legend: ~6–7 months for the most active users
With occasional use, Platinum/Legend are typically not reachable.

📊 Why this matters for NGOs

Vote weight: Higher-level supporters have stronger votes. Ten Legend votes count as sixty Basic votes.

Donation multiplier: Higher-level users generate more donation from the same purchase (Legend: ×1.25).

Discount: Users receive higher discounts at partner stores (50-100% of the max discount), which boosts buying frequency.

❓ FAQ

Do we need a separate registration?

No. If your NGO is already in the Sharity system, you are included automatically.

What if no one votes for us?

Then there is no Impact Activity donation for that month. Impact Shop purchases can still generate donations.

Is there a minimum vote count?

No. Even 1 vote qualifies you for a proportional share.

How do supporters make sure they vote for us?

Users can pick from the NGO list, or arrive from your unique link where your NGO is pre-selected.

How much is a Legend-level vote worth?

A Legend user vote counts 6x compared to Basic. At 300,000+ points, votes are heavily weighted in the distribution.
