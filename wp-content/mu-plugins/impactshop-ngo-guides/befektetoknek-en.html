💰 Business model - 3 revenue streams

Sharity is not single-channel. Revenue comes from multiple reinforcing sources.

🛒
Partner commission (Impact Shop)
500+ webshops and offline stores pay purchase commission (1-15%). Any webshop or physical store can join (QR / Partner API). From the commission, the base NGO donation share is taken first (Basic 40% · Rising 50% · Legend 60%), then multiplied by the user donation multiplier (1×-1.25×). The remainder is Sharity revenue.
Core revenue · recurring
📺
Advertising revenue (Impact Challenge)
Sponsors and advertisers pay CPM/CPA for video views and offerwall tasks (surveys, app trials, registrations). A portion flows into a quarterly donation pool distributed by vote share.
Growing · scalable
⚡
Impact Amplifier (vote packages)
Users donate directly (e.g. 500-10,000 HUF) and receive instant votes that increase NGO allocation. Accessible via NGO Card. 50% of the donation goes to NGOs, 50% covers platform costs - high-margin premium revenue.
Premium · high margin

📊 Settlement model (Direct Partner example)

Example: 10,000 HUF purchase · 5% commission → 500 HUF commission.
If NGO level is Rising (50%) and user is Gold (1.1×) → 500 × 50% × 1.1 = 275 HUF donation to the NGO.
The remaining 225 HUF is Sharity revenue. Higher levels increase donations - motivating both users and NGOs. Affiliate commissions run automatically.
